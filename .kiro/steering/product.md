# CirclePulse — Product Summary

CirclePulse is a private small-group connection app built with Flutter. Users create private groups called **Circles**, invite friends, chat, share photos, post daily photo updates called **Pulses**, and create meetup plans with simple voting.

## Core Philosophy

Private connection, not public social media. There is no public feed, no followers, no explore page, no algorithmic ranking, and no influencer metrics.

## Domain Terminology

| Product Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Circle | A private group of friends |
| Pulse | A daily photo update posted to a Circle |
| Plan | A meetup proposal with date/time and simple voting (in / maybe / can't) |

## MVP Success Criteria

A small group of friends can use the app for one week and naturally check what each other is doing, post daily updates, chat, create plans, respond to plans, and feel closer without needing a feed.
